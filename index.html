Official, MIT-licensed Piper voices selected for the English/Urdu conversational TTS pipeline. Every card below plays the exact audio produced by our test scripts, next to the exact text that was fed in.

Which models were selected, and why

Three Piper voices were selected for the final pipeline after comparing seven candidates: Ryan and Amy for English, and Fasih for Urdu. Ryan and Amy were chosen because they are the official Piper voices with the most reliable, natural output in internal listening tests — see the alternatives table below for the four other voices benchmarked and why each was set aside. Fasih was selected by necessity as well as merit: it is the only mature pre-trained Urdu voice currently available in the Piper ecosystem.

Open-source status: all three models, and the Piper engine itself (piper-tts, VITS exported to ONNX), are MIT-licensed and run entirely offline on CPU — no API keys, no per-request cost, no data leaving the machine.

Multilingual support: the pipeline covers English (US) and Urdu, routed automatically by a Unicode script-range check. Numbers, dates, and phone numbers are normalized to spoken-word form before synthesis, because Urdu digit pronunciation is otherwise unreliable (see the edge-case tests below for a before/after comparison).
